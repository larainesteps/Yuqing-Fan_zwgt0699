## Coverage gaps

Two themes in the dissertation are not well served by this list, and searching did
not turn up recent journal articles that fit:

- **OR-Tools / CP-SAT specifically.** Group B (entries 10-16) covers constraint
  programming and SAT-based scheduling, and entry 16 (Coelho and Vanhoucke) is the
  closest thing to a study of SAT solution methods for RCPSP. The CP-SAT solver
  itself is documented by Google rather than in the journal literature, so the
  existing software citation stays.
- **Priority as an objective weight versus a sort key** — the synthesis in 2.1.2 that
  sets up the 5.9 ablation. Nothing found states this contrast directly; it remains
  your own reading of the literature, which is where its value lies.
